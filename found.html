BRAYDEN MAURIELLO

Early April, 2026. College admissions start to come in. I didn’t make it.

I wondered why I wasted most of my life. My parents always said I was a smart kid, but I didn’t do anything with my “intelligence” besides feel prideful and superior. My siblings did a lot more with their time in high school than I did. Our family was always celebrating their achievements. My peers were doing incredible things, and it made me feel worthless. I was below average when compared to anyone.

I wanted to run away from home. It felt like I had nothing where I was. Maybe somewhere else I would’ve had something, would’ve been something. Maybe a different state, maybe a different country, I didn’t care. I knew I’d be able to make it on my own, I’ve always been resilient despite how pathetic I was. I wasn’t afraid of having nothing because I already had nothing. Running away from emptiness to emptiness with a hope to no longer be empty.

I wondered if anyone would miss me. My parents loved me, but I hadn’t really been present even when at home. Me and my siblings didn’t really talk. Other family I hardly ever saw or heard from. I talked to people at school, but I wouldn’t really count any of them as friends. Some people might miss me a little bit, but not a lot. They’d get over it.

I wanted to run away from home. No one would really miss me, even though I’d miss them a lot. Maybe somewhere else I’d meet people who’d miss me. Maybe a different state, maybe a different country, I didn’t care. I knew I’d be able to make it on my own, I’ve always known when people were lying to me. I wasn’t afraid of being alone because I was already alone. Running from loneliness to loneliness with a hope to no longer be alone.

I wondered if anyone would look for me. My parents would of course be looking for me. My siblings might find the task too exhausting, but they’d be there for mom and dad. Other family would be there too, they might even search, but not the way my parents would. People at school definitely wouldn’t be looking for me anywhere, it’d probably take them a while before they noticed I was missing. Some people would search for me, but they’d never find me, I know how to run.

I wanted to run away from home. Nobody would really search for me, although I’d be flatter if they did. Maybe somewhere else I’d find what I’ve been searching for. Maybe a different state, maybe a different country, I didn’t care. I knew I’d be able to make it on my own, I’ve always been able to find the things I need. I wasn’t afraid of people looking for me because I knew they’d never find me. Running away from the few people who care to places where no one cares about me with a hope to be cared for.

I never ran. I was too lazy. Things worked out in the end. Even without running, I found what I was looking for not long after all this, and it taught me how to look for it again when I’d inevitably lose it.
